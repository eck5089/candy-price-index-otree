Welcome!

In this activity, you will shop for candy under seven different price schedules. Afterward, the class will use its choices to build a fixed market basket and calculate a Candy Price Index (CaPI) and inflation rates.

🛒 How the purchasing stage works

  1. You receive a fresh 30¢ purchasing budget in every period.
  2. You must spend exactly 30¢ in each period.
  3. Prices change, so choose the bundle you genuinely prefer at each set of prices.
  4. Your budget resets each period; one choice does not reduce another period's budget.
  5. At the end, a computer draw selects one common paying period for the class.
🎲 Only the bundle you selected in the randomly chosen paying period will actually be distributed. Treat every decision as though it could be the one that counts.

⚠️ Food and allergen notice

This activity may involve chocolate, peanuts, and other food allergens. You can participate fully without receiving food. Select a non-food alternative below when appropriate, and speak with the instructor.
